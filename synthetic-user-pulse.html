Method & caveats

How to read this

One real trace of the production product was captured in a browser (screens, copy, DOM, console, network), then replayed to each of the six persona agents in isolation so all six judged an identical flow. Quantitative auth figures come from Mixpanel EU. Synthetic personas are a fast directional pass anchored in real customer quotes — the reliable signal is friction that recurs across several personas, not any single verdict. Confirm the recurring seams with live users.

To unblock Run 03, in order of value: (1) Delete the two empty "Untitled diagram" files, or give the pulse its own account — that alone restores the new-diagram path, D-01, the empty state, Templates and the progressive tips. (2) One human, ten seconds, logged out: does the hero box accept text? Closes W-02 / W-03 / W-04. (3) One human, one email login in the pane: does Turnstile now validate? It initialises again, so the Run 01 exemption ticket may not be needed at all — don't file it until this is tested. (4) Seed the account with a diagram authored by someone else, or the Knowledge Consumer is a blind spot for the third run running.
